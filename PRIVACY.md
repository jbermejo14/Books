# Privacy Policy — Libri

**Last updated: 10 August 2026**

Libri is a personal reading tracker for Android. This policy explains, plainly, what
the app does and does not do with your information.

## The short version

Libri has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your library never
leaves your device. The only thing that goes over the network is the text you type into
the search box, sent to Open Library so it can return matching books.

## What stays on your device

Everything you create in the app is stored only in Libri's private storage on your
phone:

- the books you add, and which shelf each one is on
- your reading progress
- your star ratings and your written notes

None of it is transmitted anywhere, and the developer has no way to see it. If Android
backup is enabled on your device, your operating system may include this data in your
own device backup — that is handled by Android and your Google account, not by Libri.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## What is sent over the network

When you search for a book, Libri sends **your search text** to the Open Library API,
operated by the Internet Archive, over an encrypted HTTPS connection. It also downloads
book cover images from Open Library's cover service.

These requests include a User-Agent header identifying the app and a contact email, as
Open Library asks of API clients. They do **not** include any identifier for you or your
device, and Libri does not attach any profile, account or advertising ID.

Open Library's own handling of the requests it receives is governed by the Internet
Archive's privacy policy: <https://archive.org/about/terms.php>

## What is not collected

Libri does not collect, store or transmit:

- your name, email address, or any account credentials — there are no accounts
- your location
- your contacts, photos, files, or any other app's data
- device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage analytics
- crash reports

## Permissions

Libri requests a single Android permission, `INTERNET`, used only to search Open
Library and download cover images.

## Children

Libri is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone,
including children under 13.
